Fonts bundled for the AAAVS show engine (visualizer/show-assets/fonts)

The Latin fonts and stroke fonts are the files shipped by bizarro/evangelion (app/public/fonts):
static width/weight instances generated from the Archivo and Cormorant Garamond variable fonts,
IBM Plex Mono, and the EMS / Hershey single-stroke SVG fonts. jp/ holds subsets of Noto Sans JP and
Noto Serif JP (variable, all weights kept) limited to the glyphs the NERV plates draw plus kana,
CJK punctuation, fullwidth forms, ASCII and Latin-1, made with fontTools.subset. Subsetting is a
modification permitted by the OFL; no Reserved Font Name is used.
