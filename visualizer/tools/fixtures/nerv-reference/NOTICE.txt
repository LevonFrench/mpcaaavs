NERV reference fixture

song-map.json and spectrum.bin are the audio analysis shipped by bizarro/evangelion
(https://github.com/bizarro/evangelion, data/audio.json and data/spectrum.bin) for the track
"Neon Overdrive" by mroneilovealot (Pixabay). They contain analysis data only: beats, downbeats,
sections, onsets, stem envelopes, bass pitch, and a 64-band mel spectrogram with 12 chroma bins at
100 frames per second. No audio is included.

song-map.json is data/audio.json converted to the shared song-map contract
(visualizer/src/song-map/types.ts) by visualizer/tools/make-nerv-fixture.mjs: envelopes nested under
`features`, a role and a normalized energy per section (docs/TREATMENT.md "Song map"), full confidence
and no approximations. spectrum.bin is byte-identical to upstream (SongMapBinary.spec).

Upstream does not ship data/wave.bin (it is a playable copy of the song). Renders of this fixture use a
waveform synthesized from the analysis (visualizer/src/song-map/synth-wave.ts), so every scope and
waveform trace drawn from it is APPROXIMATE: it follows the song's kicks, claps, hats, bass pitch,
chroma and envelopes, but it is not the song's signal.
